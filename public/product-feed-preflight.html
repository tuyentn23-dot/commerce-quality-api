Choose the right tool

For simple checksum checks, a local library or our free browser batch checker may suffice. For assignment/ownership lookup, use Verified by GS1; GS1 documents free single-query limits. This API provides batch pre-import issue reports, not that registry service.

Bounds and review policy

1–500 products, 1 MB maximum; text fields up to 200 characters. Keep GTIN as a string to preserve leading zeros. Duplicate ID is an error; identical GTIN plus supplied country/language and exact variants is a candidate for human review. Missing GTIN is not always an error. GTIN-8 prefixes are not reviewed; restricted/coupon prefixes are review warnings.

No full Google feed validation, ownership verification, automatic corrections/deletions or guarantee of approval. Price, availability, images and category-specific rules are outside scope. Rules informed by Google GTIN documentation; no Google affiliation.

Price and hosting

BASIC free: 100 batches/month. PRO $4.99/month: 5,000 batches, hard request limit. One batch is one request. Check listing pricing for platform bandwidth charges separately. Free hosting can sleep; retry an initial timeout after wake-up. No uptime SLA.
